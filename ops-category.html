Photos & video

Each colour has its own Add button, and whatever you put there is what a shopper sees when they choose that colour. Default is the item's general photography, shown whatever they pick — and what a colour with no pictures of its own falls back to. The first photo of all is the one that appears in listings. Short clips work too, up to a minute and 25 MB.

Sizes, stock & price

One row per colour, one column per size. Each cell holds two numbers: how many you have of that exact pairing, and what it costs. Leave the price blank and the pairing simply costs whatever the item costs — so change the price above and every blank follows it. Leave the whole thing on Default if the item does not come in colours, and use “One Size” if it does not come in sizes. A pairing left at zero stock shows as sold out rather than disappearing.

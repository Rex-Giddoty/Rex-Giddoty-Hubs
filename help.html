Placing an order

Six steps, and only the last one needs your money.

1 Find what you want. Use the search box at the top for a name you already know, or browse the category menu on the left — Collection, Bags, Shoes, Jewellery and Decor & Lifestyle. Categories with a small arrow open into sub-categories, so Collection holds Tops, Shorts, Jeans, Joggers, Fragrance and Full Fit, and Jewellery holds Watches and Necklaces.
2 Open the item. Tap the picture to see the full page: every photo, the price, what it is made of, the measurements and the sizes we have. Read the description carefully — it is where we put the detail that a photo cannot show.
3 Choose your size or option. Sizes that are sold out cannot be selected. Where only a few are left you will see how many, so you know whether to think about it or move.
4 Add it to your cart. The cart icon at the top keeps count. You can carry on shopping — nothing is lost, and you can change quantities or remove a line later from your cart.
5 Check out. Open your cart, look over the items and prices, then press Proceed to checkout. You will need an account at this point — creating one takes about a minute, and it is what lets you follow the order afterwards.
6 Give us your address and place the order. Add a delivery address and anything useful about finding it — a landmark, a gate colour, the hours somebody is home. Press Place order. Your items are held for you from that moment, and payment comes after.
You do not pay first. Placing an order reserves your items and creates an order number. Nothing is dispatched until we have confirmed your payment, and nothing is charged automatically.

How long it takes

The clock starts when your payment is confirmed, not when the order is placed. From there we pack the same day or the next working day, and the rider does the rest:

  • Lagos and other major cities — usually 1 to 3 working days.
  • Elsewhere in Nigeria — usually 3 to 7 working days, depending on how far the address is from the nearest hub.

These are the normal ranges rather than a guarantee. Public holidays, bad weather and an address nobody can find will all add time. If your order is running past its range, message us with the order number and we will chase the rider ourselves.

On the day

  • The rider will call the phone number on the order, so use one that is actually answered.
  • Open the parcel and check the item while the rider is still there.
  • If something is wrong — the wrong item, the wrong size, damage in transit — do not accept it. Tell us the same day and we will put it right.
Write a proper address. A street name and a landmark beat a description of the area every time. It is the single thing that most often turns a two-day delivery into a five-day one.

Payment

Bank transfer, confirmed by a person, before anything ships.

We take payment by bank transfer. When you place an order, the account details and the exact amount are shown on your order page and sent to you by email, so there is nothing to write down here — and nothing for anyone to fake.

1 Place the order first. You get an order number such as RG-1042 and your items are reserved.
2 Transfer the exact total to the account shown on your order page, using your order number as the transfer reference. The reference is how we match your money to your order.
3 Send us the receipt — reply to your order confirmation email with a screenshot, or message us the order number.
4 We confirm and dispatch. Confirmation is usually within a few working hours. Your order page moves from pending to paid, and you get an email at every step after that.

What we will never do

  • Ask for your card PIN, your bank password, or a one-time code.
  • Ask you to pay into a personal account that is not the one on your order page.
  • Message you from a random number asking for a "clearance" or "customs" fee.

If you are ever asked for any of these, it is not us. Stop, and contact us on the address below using the details on this site rather than the ones in the message you received.

Following your order, and changing it

Everything lives under My orders, and we email you as it moves.

Open My orders at any time to see where each order has got to. These are the stages:

pending Placed and reserved. We are waiting for your transfer.
paid Your payment has been confirmed. Packing starts.
packed Boxed and waiting for the rider.
shipped On its way to your address.
delivered Received. This is the end of the road for an order.
cancelled Called off. Nothing is owed and the items go back on sale.

Cancelling

While an order is still pending you can cancel it yourself — the button is on the order page and in My orders. Nothing is owed and the items go straight back on sale.

Once we have confirmed your payment the button goes away, because at that point there is money to send back. Message us instead and we will sort it out with you.

Changing an address or a size

Get in touch as early as you can, quoting the order number. Before the parcel leaves us this is usually easy. After it has shipped, the rider is already carrying it and the answer is normally to wait for the delivery and arrange it from there.

Common questions

The ones we are asked most.

Do I need an account to buy?

You can browse and fill a cart without one, but you need an account to check out. It takes about a minute, and it is what gives you an order history, saved addresses and a way to track and cancel orders.

Is my card charged when I place the order?

No. There is no automatic charge anywhere on this site. You place the order, then you choose to make a transfer.

How do I know my order went through?

Two ways: the order appears under My orders with a number, and a confirmation email arrives shortly after. If you see neither, the order was not created — check your cart and try again.

I did not get the email.

Check your spam or promotions folder first — a shop's first email to you often lands there. Marking it as "not spam" fixes it for good. Your orders are always visible in your account whether the email arrives or not.

Can I pay on delivery?

Not as a rule. Orders are dispatched after payment is confirmed, which is what lets us hold stock for you and keep prices where they are. If your situation is unusual, ask us before you place the order.

The item does not fit, or is not what I expected.

Tell us within 48 hours of delivery, with the order number and a photo. Unworn items in their original packaging can be exchanged for another size where we have it. Fragrance and other sealed goods can only be returned if the seal is unbroken.

Something arrived damaged or wrong.

That is on us. Do not accept it from the rider if you spot it at the door; if you only find it after, contact us the same day with a photo and we will replace it or refund you.

An item is out of stock. Will it come back?

Often, yes. Message us the item name and we will tell you whether it is being restocked and roughly when.

Still need a person?

We answer during working hours, and we do read everything.

Quoting your order number gets you a straight answer instead of three questions back.

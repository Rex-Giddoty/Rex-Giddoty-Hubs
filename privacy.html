Privacy policy

This is a plain account of what we hold about you, why we hold it, who else can see it, and how to have it removed. If anything here is unclear, ask us — the address is at the bottom.

Rex-Giddoty Hubs is a shop trading in Nigeria at rexgiddotyhubs.shop. We are the ones who decide what is collected here, and we are who to complain to.

What we collect

Only what an order or a conversation actually needs.

When you create an account

  • Your name, email address and phone number
  • A password, which is stored only as a one-way hash — nobody at this shop can read it, including us

When you place an order

  • The delivery address and phone number you give us, and any note you add
  • What you ordered, the size and colour, what it cost, and when
  • The status of the order as it moves from payment to delivery

We do not take card numbers, bank passwords or one-time codes on this site. Payment is by transfer, made in your own banking app, and the only thing that reaches us is whatever you choose to send as proof.

When you use support chat

  • What you write, and any photo, video or document you attach
  • Those files are stored privately and can only be opened by you and by our staff — never by another customer, and never by a public link

When you leave a review

  • Your rating and what you wrote, shown publicly with your first name only — never your full name, email or phone

On the device itself

  • Your cart, kept in your browser's own storage so it survives a refresh
  • Whether you prefer centimetres or inches on size guides
  • A sign-in token, so you are not asked to sign in on every page

We do not run advertising trackers, and we do not sell anything about you to anybody. There is no analytics profile of you here.

Who else sees it

Three companies, each doing one job.

  • Supabase — stores the database and the files. Your account, orders and chat live here.
  • Vercel — serves the website to your browser.
  • Resend — sends the emails: order confirmations, delivery updates, password resets.

These are processors: they hold the data so the shop can work, and they do not get to use it for their own purposes. Their servers may be outside Nigeria, which is normal for services of this kind.

We also give the delivery rider your name, address and phone number, because otherwise the parcel cannot reach you.

How long we keep it

  • Orders — kept as business records even if you close your account, because a shop has to be able to account for what it sold. They keep the address the parcel went to, not your live account.
  • Your account, saved addresses and cart — deleted when you ask.
  • Support chat and its files — deleted with your account.
  • Reviews — you can delete your own at any time, from the item.

Your choices

  • See what we hold — most of it is in your account already: your details, your addresses, every order.
  • Correct it — change your details there yourself.
  • Delete it — email us and we will remove your account, its addresses, its cart and its chat. We will tell you when it is done.
  • Stop the emails — order emails stop when the account goes. We do not send marketing email unless you subscribed to it.

You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you think we have handled your information badly.

Children

This shop is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect anything about a child under 13. If you believe a child has created an account here, tell us and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it and we say so on the site. The current version is always the one on this page.
